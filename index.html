How it works

Every time you buy or swap a token on Solana, a tiny account is created that locks about 0.002 SOL as rent. If you trade even 2–3 tokens a day or week, those leftover accounts stay on-chain and the SOL stays locked forever — until you close them.

This tool only closes accounts that have exactly zero tokens. It never transfers, approves, or burns any token. Keys never leave your browser. Zero fee.

  1. Connect Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or any Solana wallet.
  2. Scan shows empty accounts that can be closed.
  3. Uncheck anything you want to keep, then click Reclaim Selected and confirm in your wallet.

If your wallet ever asks to approve or transfer any token (USDC etc.), reject it. This tool never does that.
