How it works (for beginners)

Every time you receive a token on Solana, a small account is created that locks ~0.002 SOL as rent. When the account is empty, that SOL is still locked until you close it.

  1. Connect your Phantom wallet (we never see your keys).
  2. Click Scan Wallet to find empty accounts and tiny dust balances.
  3. Click Reclaim All. Confirm in Phantom. Your SOL returns to your wallet.
